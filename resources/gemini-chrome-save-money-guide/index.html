Part 01 · Setup

Use Gemini with your open tabs

Open the pages involved in one task, then share only the tabs Gemini needs.

01Open Gemini in Chrome

Update Chrome, sign in, open the first useful webpage, then click Ask Gemini at the top of the browser. Follow the onscreen opt-in instructions the first time.

Official Google demonstration. Source: Google Chrome product blog.
02Add the relevant tabs

Open the other pages you need. In Gemini, click Add tabs, or type @, and select up to 10 open tabs. Shared tabs display a check mark.

Official Google demonstration. Source: Google Chrome product blog.
03Give Gemini a structured task

Use one of the prompts below. Replace everything inside brackets and ask Gemini to identify the source tab for every important claim.

Official Google demonstration. Source: Google Chrome product blog.
04Check the answer at its source

Use Gemini’s answer to narrow the work, then reopen the original tabs and verify decisive prices, dates, policies and conditions.

Official Google demonstration. Source: Google Chrome product blog.
Can’t see Ask Gemini? Google is rolling the feature out gradually. You need an eligible signed-in account, a supported region, device and language, the latest Chrome, and a normal window rather than Incognito.
Part 02 · Workflows

Six useful jobs for your open tabs

Each workflow begins with a familiar situation and ends with details you should verify yourself.

Workflow 01

Compare before buying

You have several product tabs open and every option claims to be the best.

Open: product pages, delivery information, warranty terms and return policies.
Compare the products in these tabs for someone who needs [describe your normal use]. Create a table covering: - Total price, including delivery and required accessories - Important differences - Warranty and return policy - Recurring or hidden costs - Repeated complaints found in the shared pages Recommend the best overall option, not simply the cheapest one. Cite the supporting tab for every price and important claim. Mark missing information as unknown.
Verify yourself: checkout price, seller identity, delivery date, warranty exclusions and return window.
Workflow 02

Plan a trip across multiple tabs

Your flight, hotel and activity options are spread across different pages.

Open: flights, hotels, transport, attractions and cancellation policies.
Use these tabs to create a practical trip plan for [number of people] travelling to [destination] from [dates]. Include: - Flight and hotel costs - Baggage, taxes and additional fees - Airport and local transport - Travel time between activities - Cancellation conditions - Any cost or booking detail that is still missing Present a day-by-day itinerary and an estimated total. Cite the source tab for every price and schedule.
Verify yourself: currency, baggage allowance, local taxes, transfer time and cancellation deadlines.
Workflow 03

Understand a subscription

The introductory price looks attractive, but the conditions are scattered across several pages.

Open: pricing, plan comparison, features, terms and cancellation pages.
Compare these subscription plans based on what I will actually pay during the first year and after the introductory period. I mainly need it for [describe your use]. Show: - Monthly and annual cost - Price after promotions end - Contract length - Cancellation requirements - Important limitations - Features I am unlikely to need Recommend the most sensible plan and cite the relevant tab for every cost and condition.
Verify yourself: renewal date, trial conversion, annual commitment and cancellation method.
Workflow 04

Turn recipes into one meal plan

You found several recipes but need one realistic plan and shopping list.

Open: recipe pages and, optionally, a supermarket page.
Use these recipes to create a [number]-day meal plan for [number of people]. Our preferences or restrictions are: [add details]. Ingredients we already have: [add ingredients]. Create: - A simple daily meal plan - One combined shopping list grouped by category - Quantities adjusted for the number of people - Practical ingredient substitutions - A plan for reusing leftovers Cite the recipe tab used for each meal.
Verify yourself: allergies, serving sizes, cooking times and safe food storage.
Workflow 05

Understand a long document

You need the important information without reading every paragraph first.

Open: the article, report, policy or terms page.
Explain this document in plain language. Organise the answer into: - What this document is about - The five most important points - Anything that affects me directly - Dates, costs, obligations or deadlines - Actions I may need to take - Statements that are unclear or require confirmation Quote only short essential phrases and point me to the relevant section of the page for each important claim.
Verify yourself: deadlines, legal or financial obligations, eligibility and the original wording of decisive clauses.
Workflow 06

Make sense of several reviews

Reviews disagree and you cannot tell which complaints actually matter.

Open: the official product page and several independent reviews.
Compare the official product claims with the reviews in these tabs. Separate the findings into: - Strengths mentioned repeatedly - Problems mentioned repeatedly - Isolated complaints - Claims supported by testing - Marketing claims that were not independently verified - The type of person this product suits - The type of person who should avoid it Do not treat every opinion as equally reliable. Cite the supporting tab for every conclusion and explain any disagreement between reviewers.
Verify yourself: review date, tested model, sponsorship disclosures, regional differences and long-term ownership evidence.
Part 03 · Before you share

Protect your privacy and verify the result

Your current tab may be shared by default. Review the shared-tab list before every prompt.
✓Close banking, passwords, private messages, medical information and confidential work pages.
✓Remove every tab Gemini does not need.
✓Open Chrome Settings → AI innovations → Gemini in Chrome to manage permissions and the default tab-sharing setting.
✓Confirm every decisive price, deadline, policy and condition on the original page.
